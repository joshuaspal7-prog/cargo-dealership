About Car-Go

A dealership built on passion, family and trust.

Car-Go Dealership Inc. was started by a young entrepreneur who has loved cars for as long as he can remember. Growing up, he watched his father run a car dealership back in Syria, and he rolled up his sleeves early, helping maintain the vehicles and sell them to customers.

Those years taught him that a great dealership is about more than cars. It’s about people. His passion only grew stronger with time, and it led him to open Car-Go Dealership Inc. here in Hamilton.

Our mission is simple: sell quality cars with honesty and transparency, and give every customer a good vibe, so everyone who walks through our door feels welcomed and safe.
